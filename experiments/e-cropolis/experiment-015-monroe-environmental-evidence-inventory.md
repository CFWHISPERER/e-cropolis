# E-Cropolis Experiment 015 — Monroe Environmental Evidence Inventory

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** Protocol established; evidence collection pending

## Objective

Identify and evaluate real environmental evidence that could support an ecologically meaningful simulation of a hypothetical 650-acre settlement near Monroe, Oregon.

Determine which landscape characteristics can be represented using documented observations rather than invented simulation assumptions.

## Research Question

What environmental data are available for the Monroe-area landscape, and what are their spatial, temporal and epistemic limitations?

## Method

### 1. Define the study area

Identify a real watershed or subwatershed near Monroe, Oregon, suitable for environmental investigation.

Record its official name, boundary source and geographic extent.

Keep the fictional 650-acre E-Cropolis property distinct from real parcels and land ownership.

### 2. Inventory candidate evidence

Investigate six environmental layers:

| Layer | Research focus | Candidate source |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrology | Watersheds, streams and drainage networks | USGS |
| Soils | Soil classes, drainage and water-holding characteristics | USDA NRCS |
| Terrain | Elevation, slope and aspect | USGS 3DEP |
| Climate | Rainfall, temperature and seasonal variation | PRISM Climate Group |
| Vegetation | Land cover, woodland and agricultural landscapes | USGS |
| Wetlands | Wetland distribution and hydrological relationships | USFWS |

These are candidate sources, not yet verified datasets for the study area.

### 3. Record provenance

For each selected dataset, document:

- Publishing institution and dataset name
- Source URL and access date
- Dataset version or publication date
- Spatial resolution and geographic coverage
- Temporal coverage, where applicable
- Coordinate reference system, where applicable
- Known limitations and uncertainty
- Relevance to potential ecological modeling

### 4. Separate evidence from interpretation

Classify statements as:

- **Observed:** Supported directly by documented measurements or mapped evidence
- **Inferred:** Derived from observations using an explicit method
- **Hypothetical:** Proposed interventions or processes not yet established for this landscape
- **Simulated:** Outputs generated by an implemented computational model

Do not present simulated behavior as ecological evidence.

1. A reproducible geographic study boundary is established.
2. Candidate datasets are assessed for coverage, resolution and provenance.
3. Relevant limitations and uncertainties are documented.
4. At least one ecological process is identified as suitable for a subsequent modeling experiment, with a defensible evidence basis.
