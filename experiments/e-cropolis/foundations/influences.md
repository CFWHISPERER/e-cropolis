# E-Cropolis — Foundational Influences and Ethos

**Version:** 0.1 (working draft)  
**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Purpose

E-Cropolis investigates settlements that become **increasingly alive, aligned, and enjoyable**, cultivating regenerative capability rather than progressively liquidating natural capital. This document records influences and hypotheses, not experimentally verified outcomes.

## Emerging settlement hypotheses

- **Regenerative capability:** Measure whether soils, water, biodiversity, seed diversity, and future productivity are preserved or enlarged, including impacts beyond settlement boundaries.
- **Enjoyable sufficiency:** Design comfortable, accessible, adaptable homes and welcoming shared landscapes without equating larger private floor area with better living.
- **Human-scale density:** Explore a provisional five-story building maximum while testing effects on land use, materials, accessibility, and ecological habitat.
- **Accessible mobility:** Prioritize walking, cycling, and efficient public transport, electric where appropriate, over car-dependent design.
- **Distributed work:** Support remote work when practical, shared neighborhood workspaces, and the essential value of place-based work.
- **Water stewardship:** Explore rain gardens, infiltration, soil cover, appropriate storage, and watershed-sensitive design.
- **Regenerative materials:** Study coppiced and pollarded willow and other renewable construction materials, accounting for nutrient exports, processing, and service life.
- **Accountable computation:** Assess appropriately scaled 'data-decentres' by useful capability and full energy, water, materials, land, and community impacts.
- **RRI-informed incentives:** Test credits for verified food, pollinator, soil, water, seed, and material contributions while protecting fairness, habitat, and public finances.

## RRI and epistemic integrity

**Productivity is not automatically regeneration.** Crop yields, material harvests, or visible greenery may coexist with depletion. RRI remains a developing framework; indicators, weights, baselines, and causal claims require validation.

Distinguish **observations, design preferences, hypotheses, model assumptions, and reproduced experimental results**. Account for externalized effects, disclose uncertainty, and preserve negative findings. The successful reproducibility test in Experiment 010 establishes a limited technical capability, **not** the ecological performance of a proposed settlement.

## Guiding proposition

> E-Cropolis explores settlements increasingly alive, aligned, and enjoyable—organized around cultivating regenerative capability rather than progressively liquidating natural capital.

Two recurring questions:

1. **What living capabilities remain—or have been enlarged—for future generations and other organisms?**
2. **Would people genuinely want to live there?**

This is a living reference, to be revised as inquiry and experiments progress.
